About Me

I’m Mahmoud, A Graphic Designer And Ui/ux Designer With Over 3 Years Of Professional Experience In Visual Design. I Specialize In Transforming Ideas Into Meaningful Visual Experiences That Combine Creativity, Strategy, And Clear Communication.
throughout My Career, I’ve Worked Across Branding, Social Media Design, Logo Design, And Print Design, Developing A Strong Understanding Of Visual Hierarchy, Typography, Composition, Color, And Visual Storytelling. I’m Proficient In Adobe Photoshop And Adobe Illustrator, Which I Use To Create Impactful And Polished Visual Solutions.
currently, I’m Expanding My Expertise Into Ui/ux Design, Bringing My Strong Visual Design Background Into The Digital Product Space. I Use Figma To Create Clean, Intuitive, And User-centered Interfaces, While Focusing On Usability, Structure, User Flows, And Seamless Digital Experiences.
i Believe Great Design Is More Than Just Aesthetics — It’s About Solving Problems, Communicating Ideas, And Creating Experiences That People Can Understand, Use, And Remember.
